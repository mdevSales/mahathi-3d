About

Making the complex feel simple.

I'm a Principal Sales Engineer in Salesforce's Global Public Sector org and an SME in Agentforce & Salesforce AI — which is a formal way of saying I help government and nonprofit teams cut through the AI hype and put agents to work on the missions that matter.

My favorite part isn't the tech — it's the moment the room gets it. I take genuinely hard architecture and turn it into a story people can act on. (Yes, I'm a yapper. It turns out that's a useful skill.)

Outside the demos, I mentor engineers coming up behind me, champion nonprofits, and stay close to my South Asian community — because the best technology is the kind that lifts the people doing the hardest work.
