Why I do this

Technology should serve the people who serve everyone else.

I believe the organizations doing the most important work — government agencies, nonprofits, the public sector — are too often handed the most complicated tools. That's backwards. When the mission is hard, the technology should be the easy part.

So that's the work I chose. As a Principal Sales Engineer in Salesforce's Global Public Sector org and an SME in Agentforce & Salesforce AI, I don't sell software — I earn trust. I take genuinely hard AI architecture and make it clear enough that a room full of people can see exactly how it changes their day. The moment they get it is the whole job.

And I don't do it alone. I mentor the engineers coming up behind me, champion the nonprofits punching above their weight, and stay rooted in my South Asian community — because technology only matters if it lifts the people doing the hardest work.
